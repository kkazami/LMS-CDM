# CdM LMS policy review

The Terms and Conditions (`/terms`) and Privacy Policy (`/privacy`) were drafted from the current account/roster workflows, Prisma models, session controls, academic modules, assessment integrity tools, gamification, Knowledge Exchange, GitHub, managed execution, AI, and device-storage implementation on 21 October 2026. `/support` uses the existing campus address and Registrar referral process; no email address, telephone number, or named privacy officer was invented.

Before institutional publication, the school administration and designated privacy officer should confirm:

- The responsible controller's official name, campus address, direct DPO/privacy contact, helpdesk channel, and handling procedure for rights requests and security incidents. Replace Registrar referral guidance with direct verified contacts when available.
- The legal basis for each processing purpose, especially sensitive academic records, integrity monitoring, minors, optional integrations, and any separate consent or guardian authorization.
- The deployed providers, data locations, international transfers, provider agreements, and any required supplementary notices. Code supports integrations that may not be enabled in every deployment.
- The approved retention and disposal schedule for academic records, account/session/audit records, integrity events, email delivery logs, stored files, local caches, and backups. The notice intentionally does not invent fixed periods or promise automated deletion.
- The institution's academic review, discipline, accessibility, incident response, and material policy change procedures.

The required `acceptedPolicies: true` field is checked for every new password login, including mobile and desktop clients. It records an acknowledgement in the request and does not create a permanent, versioned acceptance receipt. Existing sessions remain subject to their normal expiry; this change does not retroactively collect acceptance from them. If the school requires proof of acceptance or forced reacceptance after updates, that needs a separate approved retention/versioning design.
